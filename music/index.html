Jake Huryn's music

  • an album of ambient electronica using Logic Pro (~30 min);
  • a short microtonal piece created using Logic Pro (specifically, in 22edo; <1 min).
If we are near a piano or keyboard, I will be very glad to perform my piano pieces, although I my playing can be somewhat clumsy.
